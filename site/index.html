01 Listen Speech recognition

  1. Who does it: speech recognition

    We find every spoken word and the moment it is said. That shows where the pauses are.

  2. Who does it: Gemma 4

    Gemma looks at frames from each scene and lists only what it can see, including text on screen.

  3. Who does it: code

    Code picks which pauses get a description and how many words fit. If a pause is too short, the video can stop for a moment.

  4. Who does it: Gemma 4

    Gemma writes each line to fit its pause. It also writes the easy-read captions and the Hindi and Italian versions.

  5. Who does it: code, then Gemma repairs

    Code checks every line: timing, length, names nobody said, guesses about feelings or jobs. Gemma fixes what fails, and code checks it again.

  6. Who does it: a person

    A person approves what the public hears. Anything not yet approved is marked as a draft.
