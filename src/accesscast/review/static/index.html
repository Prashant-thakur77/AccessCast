            Keys

            Keys work anywhere except while you type in a text field. Press Esc in a text field to go back to the list of lines. Use Enter to press a button.

            Keyboard shortcuts
            KeyWhat it does
            SpacePlay or pause the video
            , and .One frame back or forward
            [ and ]Previous or next line
            aApprove
            rReject
            nNeeds revision
            eEdit the final text
            pPlay the line in context, 2 seconds either side
            hHear it: make the voice for the current text and play it with 2 seconds of the video before and after
            lJump to the waiting line with the lowest confidence
            iAdd a description line of your own, starting where the video is now
            ?Open this list
            EscClose this list, or leave a text field
